About the Audit Log

Every contribution is recorded as a tamper-evident entry using SHA256 hash chaining and HMAC signatures.

Modifying, deleting, or inserting any record breaks the chain — detected immediately by the verification scanner.

This provides cryptographic proof that all records are genuine and unmodified.
